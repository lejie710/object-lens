Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE high-fidelity desktop web application design concept for the approved project 'Object Lens'. No laptop/device mockup, no perspective, no decorative environment outside the app. The intended user is someone selecting one photo and inspecting object-detection results. The design must feel like a carefully made everyday image utility: calm, precise, human-designed, modest, easy to use. Below, a workspace toolbar: filename 'desk.jpg' and small '1600 × 1200' at left, a blue rectangular 'Choose image' button and quiet 'Clear' button at right. Main workspace is an approximately 75/25 split: a large photographic image pane on the left and a narrow white results panel on the right. Only one vertical divider, useful whitespace, clean alignment, very few enclosing borders. The right panel is a list, not separate cards.

Photo content: realistic ordinary desk in natural daylight, containing exactly a dark open laptop, a ceramic coffee cup, and a clear reusable water bottle. Natural wood desk and neutral room, crisp photographic detail, no brand logos, no stylized 3D graphics. Overlay three accurate thin object detection rectangles around those three objects. The laptop is selected, shown with a clear blue outline and a small blue 'Laptop' label. Cup and bottle use quieter teal and ochre outlines with tiny readable labels. These are purely illustrative visual annotations.

Results panel: heading 'Objects' with a small count '3'. Small column caption 'Model score'. Bottom of the workspace, a small status line with an unobtrusive check symbol and 'Detection complete'. At the very bottom outside the functional content, tiny unobtrusive caption 'Design concept · Illustrative results'. All copy must be English and spelled accurately.
